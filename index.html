OEOSOLUTION FACTORY

Organizational Effectiveness Office

Not a company department — a repeatable operating loop. OEO continuously diagnoses the world's business and workforce pain points, ranks them by opportunity, resolves the top ones into short solution briefs, and hands them to the team to develop, test, and either scale or retire.

01
Diagnose

AI monitors global sources — search trends, forums, funding data, job markets, complaint volume — for recurring business and employee pain points, worldwide.

02
Resolve

Each problem is scored on demand, feasibility, GTM difficulty and competitive white-space, ranked, then distilled into a short solution brief with TAM/SAM and a develop/no-develop grade.

03
Develop

The team builds the smallest testable version of the top-graded solution — this is the human, hands-on stage the loop feeds.

04
Track

Live KPIs, defined before launch, determine pass/fail. Winners scale toward a sale of equity to an entrepreneur or investor; losers are archived as signal.

The loop restarts. Exit proceeds and lessons from Track feed back into Diagnose — new capital and new signal, same factory.
Wiring this up live: data on this page is a seeded demo dataset, not a live feed. To connect it to your OpenAI key on GitHub, set CONFIG.OPENAI_API_KEY and CONFIG.USE_LIVE_AI = true at the top of the script in index.html. Keep the key out of a public repo — use a private repo or a small serverless proxy so the key isn't exposed client-side.
